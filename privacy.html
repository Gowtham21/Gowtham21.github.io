Privacy policy

SolVettai (சொல்வேட்டை), a Tamil word game for Android, by Gowtham21. Effective 10 October 2026.

Summary

This version of SolVettai does not collect, send or share any personal data. You play without an account, the game has no advertising and no analytics, and everything you do in the game stays on your phone.

What the game keeps, and where

DataWhere it staysWhy
Your progress: levels, scores, coins, hints, achievements, the player name you typeOn your phone onlySo you can continue playing
Settings: language, sound, vibration, input styleOn your phone onlyTo remember your choices
Technical notes about a crash, if one happensOn your phone only; not sent anywhereTo help fix problems in later versions

The game is not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server for this version and cannot see your progress or your name.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's backup on your phone, Android may include the game's progress and settings in your personal Google backup so that they come back when you change phones. That backup is made by Android under your Google account and Google's privacy policy; we do not receive it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Purchases and advertising

This version shows no advertising and sells nothing. If a later version offers purchases, payments are handled by Google Play; we never receive your card or payment details. This policy will be updated before such a version is released.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions that give access to personal information (no contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files). It uses vibration for feedback, and the network and start-up permissions are reserved for optional online features that are switched off in this version.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone but is not designed for children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Because everything stays on your phone, uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings (Settings → Apps → சொல்வேட்டை → Storage → Clear data), deletes it completely.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting or sending data — for example online accounts, cloud save or multiplayer — this policy will be updated first, with the new effective date at the top.
